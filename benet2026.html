12th BELGIAN NETWORK RESEARCH MEETING

The 12th edition of the Belgian Network Research Meeting (BENet) will be organised by the Complex Systems Institute from Ghent University on December 11, 2026. This edition will also see the participation of COMPLEX, the FNRS Doctoral School on Complex Systems. The purpose of this annual gathering is to foster communication among Belgian researchers employing network science on their work, regardless of their discipline: sociology, economics, communication, history, biology, physics, medicine, informatics, etc.

    Call for Abstracts

    We welcome the submission of short research abstracts. Authors of selected contributions will be invited to present and discuss their work during the meeting. Please take into account the interdisciplinary nature of this event: tutorial presentations will be favored over detailed reports. If you prefer a shorter (5 min) or longer (15 min) talk, specify your choice during the submission. We will try to accommodate all wishes.

    Submit here your title and abstract (text only, 180-200 words) by November 13, 2026.
